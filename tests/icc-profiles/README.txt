The four files retain their upstream Git blob IDs and are used only by tests.
They are not included in the viewer's installer or app bundle.

The AdobeCompat profiles provide Adobe RGB compatible matrix/TRC data.
DisplayP3 profiles provide Display P3 matrix/TRC data.
Both version 2 and version 4 encodings are tested against LittleCMS.
These fixtures do not establish coverage for every ICC LUT/CMYK profile.
